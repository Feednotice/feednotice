# Name and logo

The code is free software under the AGPL. The **FeedNotice name and logo are not**: they identify
the service run at feednotice.com, and readers who get an email "via FeedNotice" must be able to
trust who sent it, who holds their address and whom to ask to be removed.

That is why this repository contains no logo, and why the software runs under a neutral name
until you give it yours (`SERVICE_NAME`).

## What you can do

- Run the code, change it and share it, under the AGPL.
- Run it privately, or inside your organisation, as it is.
- Say that your service is "based on FeedNotice" or "runs FeedNotice software", in plain text.
- Keep the technical names that make the software work: the `FeedNotice/…` User-Agent product
  token, the `X-FeedNotice-Ref` header, the publisher proof files (`feednotice.txt`,
  `_feednotice` records).

## What needs your own name and logo

- A service open to the public: give it its own name (`SERVICE_NAME`), and, if you want one,
  its own logo ([docs/branding.md](docs/branding.md)).
- Do not suggest that your service is feednotice.com, or run or endorsed by its operator.
- Do not use a logo or name that could be confused with FeedNotice's.
